## 1. FEATURE SPECIFICATION & DATA SOURCE MAPPING
The application must fetch and render real-time weather data using the specific tagged providers tailored for accuracy in the Ohio River Valley microclimate:

1. Core Conditions & Severe Weather Alerts:
   - Primary Source: National Weather Service (NWS) / NOAA API.
   - Anchors: Station KCVG (Cincinnati/NKY International Airport) and KLUK (Cincinnati Municipal Airport / Lunken Field).
   - Metrics: Current temperature, "Feels Like", wind speed/direction, humidity, dew point, pressure, and real-time NWS push alert payloads (Severe Thunderstorm, Tornado, Flash Flood warnings).

2. Minute-Cast & Short-Term Precipitation:
   - Primary Source: OpenWeather One Call API / Tomorrow.io NOWCAST feed.
   - Requirements: 60-minute hyper-local minute-by-minute rain intensity forecast and hourly breakdown for 24-48 hours.

3. Interactive Radar & Map Overlays:
   - Primary Source: NOAA NEXRAD raw feeds via Iowa Environmental Mesonet (IEM) / RainViewer API tile wrappers.
   - Anchor Station: KILN (Wilmington, OH NEXRAD Radar Sweep).
   - Layers: Standard precipitation radar, Doppler velocity sweeps, wind streamlines, and visible/IR satellite overlays.

4. Pollen & Allergy Tracking:
   - Primary Source: Pollen.com / Regional Sampling Station Data.
   - Metrics: Daily regional allergen index broken down into Tree, Grass, and Weed pollen levels.

5. Air Quality Index (AQI):
   - Primary Source: AirNow API (US EPA) / Southwest Ohio & NKY Air Quality Network.
   - Metrics: AQI score, PM2.5, Ozone, and health advice alerts.

6. Sun, Moon & Solar Dynamics:
   - Primary Source: Local Ephemeris / SunCalc algorithmic calculations (Coordinate-based ~38.9° N, 84.5° W).
   - Metrics: Sunrise, Sunset, Golden Hour, Blue Hour, Moon phase, and percentage illumination.
